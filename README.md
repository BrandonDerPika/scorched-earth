# Scorched Earth

**Speedrun the destruction of Earth.**

Scorched Earth is a strategy game where you race to industrialize the
planet, exploit its resources, and accelerate environmental destruction
as quickly as possible. Starting in 1800, your goal is to bring about
global overheating in record time.

Collect resources, purchase upgrades, and expand industrial production
to maximize pollution. Watch as the planet progressively deteriorates
through increasingly ridiculous graphics.

## Gameplay

The game revolves around gathering resources and investing them into
upgrades to accelerate industrialization.

-   **Gather Resources:** Collect Grain, Coal, Iron, and Oil to fuel
    industrial expansion.
-   **Purchase Upgrades:** Improve resource production and accelerate
    your progress.
-   **Build Factories:** Expand industrial capacity to increase
    pollution.
-   **Destroy the Planet:** Race to make Earth uninhabitable in the
    shortest time possible.

## Game Jam

Developed for the PolyMars x ScoreSpace Game Jam, a 72-hour game jam
hosted by PolyMars and ScoreSpace.

The game explores the theme through a satirical take on
industrialization and humanity's relationship with the environment.
